# EIF Programme 4.1 — Copilot at Work: Applied Productivity (V4)

**Audience:** Foundation / Foundation+ — early-career banking employees, new hires and interns.  
**Format:** Guided demonstrations and individual exercises using synthetic Crescent Bay Bank content.  
**Duration:** **13.5 instructional hours over two days** (Day 1: 8 hours; Day 2: 5.5 hours).  
**Hands-on:** 30 progressive labs, **575 of 810 minutes (71%)** devoted to learner activities. No coding.  
**Requirements:** Eligible Microsoft 365 Copilot features in supported work applications, provisioned learner accounts and seeded training content. Live capability testing is required before delivery.

## 1. Overall learning design

Learn by doing: discover the work Copilot experience, practise structured prompting, draft and compare supported documents, review training emails and meetings, interpret basic operational data, and present verified conclusions. Each lab is self-contained. Optional modules on responsible AI and a capstone are retained outside the core.

## 2. Learning outcomes

Participants will: write and refine prompts; draft clear workplace communication; summarise and compare source files; recognise tentative decisions in correspondence and meetings; examine synthetic operational data; prepare a brief presentation; and verify AI output for factual, numerical, privacy and approval risks.

## 4. Hands-on learning architecture

- **Start:** introduce one business question and its source.
- **Practise:** write a first prompt; use Copilot on synthetic content.
- **Refine:** request a shorter, better-grounded or more useful response.
- **Verify:** check claims against their source; identify approval and privacy limitations.
- **Save:** keep a short evidence file with the lab ID.

Thirty labs progress from a first work request to source-based communication, analysis and management reporting. Learners can use labelled fallback source materials when a prior exercise or tenant capability is unavailable. A fallback exercise is not proof that live Copilot functioned.

## 5. Responsible AI as a continuous practice

Accuracy, privacy, approval and source reliability are checked within L09, L15, L18, L21, L24, L27 and L30, rather than as a separate core module.

## 6. Learner and environment prerequisites

Basic computer and Office document skills; no coding. Each learner requires a laptop, internet access, a provisioned organisational account and appropriate licensed Copilot capabilities. The training provider must seed approved sample files, two fictional email threads and a Teams meeting with the required recording/transcription options where permitted. Copilot app, Word, Outlook, OneDrive, Teams, Excel, PowerPoint and Notebooks must be individually tested by EIF before the programme. Application control names may differ across tenants and release channels.

## 7. Learning materials and assessment

Learner instructions, synthetic Word/Excel/PowerPoint/email/meeting resources, reusable prompt recipe, guided source-based lab fallbacks, Day 1 knowledge check, final assessment and attendance/assessment tracker. Instructor-only demos and answer keys are distributed separately.

**Delivery status:** All written materials are designed for the EIF environment but have not been tested inside its Microsoft 365 tenant. Day-0 feature sign-off is a delivery gate.
