## Prompt practice for turn an email thread into an action register

1. **Starter:** “Summarise the actions in this training email thread.” Enter this with the synthetic document open; replace any names in brackets with facts from the named source.
2. **Improve:** “Separate confirmed owners and deadlines from proposed dates.” Compare the second response with the first, retaining your own prompt edits.
3. **Check:** “Which action or deadline is missing evidence?” Do a manual source check before accepting the response.
