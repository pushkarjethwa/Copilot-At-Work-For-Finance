## Prompt practice for turn a brief into slide outline

1. **Starter:** “Outline four slides for an onboarding improvement update.” Enter this with the synthetic document open; replace any names in brackets with facts from the named source.
2. **Improve:** “Make each slide headline a decision-relevant statement.” Compare the second response with the first, retaining your own prompt edits.
3. **Check:** “Which slide would need evidence before publication?” Do a manual source check before accepting the response.
