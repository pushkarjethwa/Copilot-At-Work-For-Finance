# Responsible AI — four checks before using an output

- **Accuracy:** Does every factual or numerical claim match a reliable, relevant provided file?
- **Privacy:** Does the draft avoid real customer information, internal risk details and sharing without permission?
- **Approval:** Is this only a draft or has the authorised owner approved it for the proposed audience?
- **Source:** Is the document current, effective and approved? Are uncertain items explicitly marked?

Stop and request human review if you cannot verify any of the four. Copilot can draft and summarise, but it cannot authorise a banking decision.
