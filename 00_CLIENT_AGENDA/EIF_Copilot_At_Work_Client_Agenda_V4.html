01 / Learning design

Begin with a task

Start with everyday workplace writing, research and preparation. Make prompting a habit—not a copy-and-paste trick.

Work from evidence

Use synthetic documents, email and meetings, then analyse banking operations and create a verified management story.

02 / Learning outcomes

Write and improve prompts. Draft and summarise approved source material. Interpret messages and meetings. Analyse basic Excel metrics. Build a short presentation. Check facts, figures, privacy and approval status.
