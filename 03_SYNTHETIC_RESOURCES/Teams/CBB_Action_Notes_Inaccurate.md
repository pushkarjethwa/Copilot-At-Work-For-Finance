# Inaccurate meeting recap — synthetic training decoy

- Downtown **definitely** has the worst late percentage. [UNVERIFIED]
- All CBB applications must be approved within two days. [FALSE]
- Sara can send the draft response without compliance approval. [FALSE]
- The new checklist **will launch Monday**. [UNAPPROVED]
- Omar will calculate the KPIs by Thursday afternoon. [SUPPORTED as a stated commitment]

Learners should identify which statements have clear meeting support and which should not be repeated.
