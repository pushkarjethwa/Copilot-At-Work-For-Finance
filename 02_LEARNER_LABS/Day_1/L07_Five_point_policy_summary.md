## Prompt practice for five-point policy summary

1. **Starter:** “Summarise the approved onboarding policy in five points.” Enter this with the synthetic document open; replace any names in brackets with facts from the named source.
2. **Improve:** “Add the effective date and distinguish targets from guarantees.” Compare the second response with the first, retaining your own prompt edits.
3. **Check:** “Which point can be traced directly to the approved source?” Do a manual source check before accepting the response.
