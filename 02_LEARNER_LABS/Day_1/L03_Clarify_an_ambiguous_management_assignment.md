## Prompt practice for clarify an ambiguous management assignment

1. **Starter:** “What is clear and unclear in this manager request?” Enter this with the synthetic document open; replace any names in brackets with facts from the named source.
2. **Improve:** “Put assumptions in a separate section and ask three questions.” Compare the second response with the first, retaining your own prompt edits.
3. **Check:** “Which claim has no evidence in the document?” Do a manual source check before accepting the response.
